Powering Electrification with Innovative Waste Heat Solutions

We design, manufacture, install, and service cutting-edge Organic Rankine Cycle (ORC) power systems, converting waste heat into clean, reliable, and cost-effective electricity. Our solutions empower hard-to-abate sectors and remote communities to achieve greater energy efficiency and sustainability.

Our scalable systems, ranging from 100 kW to 10 MW, help customers cut energy costs, reduce emissions, and enhance energy resilience. At Cardinal Volta, we’re shaping a sustainable energy future by transforming waste heat into a critical resource for electrification.
